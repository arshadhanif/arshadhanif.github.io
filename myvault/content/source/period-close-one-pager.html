Product overview

Oracle Fusion Cloud Period Close Accelerator

A ready to use, configurable checklist that takes the guesswork out of your monthly close.

Most finance teams running Oracle Fusion close late and close anxiously, because the knowledge of how the close should run lives in one or two people's heads. This pack puts that knowledge into a single, structured workbook your whole team can follow, every month, in the right order.

What you get

100+ sequenced tasks Every close task across 12 Financials and SCM modules, in dependency order, each with the exact Oracle ESS job or report name and clear recommendations.
Scope driven Mark each module In Scope or Out of Scope once. Tasks, reports and known issues for modules you do not use grey out across the whole file automatically.
Known issues built in A library of the real pitfalls and dependency traps that delay closes, with an explanation of why each one happens and how to avoid it.
100+ standard reports Every Oracle delivered reconciliation report, tagged by module, purpose and account type. Register your own custom reports and they flow in automatically.
Live close tracker Assign owners and target days, and watch completion roll up across the close as tasks are marked done. No manual status chasing.
Built to reuse Client neutral and fully configurable. Set it up once per client and run it month after month. Nothing to install, works in standard Excel.

Who it is for

Finance controllers, ERP consultants and implementation partners who want a faster, cleaner, audit ready close in Oracle Fusion Cloud, and who want the close process to survive staff turnover.
