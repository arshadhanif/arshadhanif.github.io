ERP consulting sits at the intersection of finance knowledge and technology
delivery — and it pays accordingly. If you have an accounting or finance
background, you are closer than you think. Here is the roadmap I wish I had.

## Step 1: Pick a platform

Oracle Fusion, SAP, and Workday dominate the enterprise market. Specialising
beats being a generalist early on. I chose **Oracle Fusion Financials** because
it leans heavily on the accounting expertise I already had.

## Step 2: Build real configuration skills

Reading documentation is not enough. Get hands-on:

- Request a trial or training environment.
- Configure a full procure-to-pay or record-to-report cycle end to end.
- Break things on purpose, then fix them.

## Step 3: Certify

Certifications open doors and get you past procurement filters. An **Oracle
certification** plus a finance qualification like **ACCA** is a powerful
combination — it signals both the *what* and the *why*.

## Step 4: Get on a project

Your first implementation is the real qualification. Networks matter here — being
part of a strong professional network (mine ran through **PwC**) is often how the
first project lands.

## Step 5: Develop the soft skills

The best consultants translate between finance and IT. Workshops, requirement
gathering, and stakeholder management are where careers are actually made.

> The technical skills get you in the room. The communication skills keep you
> there — and get you invited back.

## A realistic timeline

Expect 12–18 months from a standing start to your first billable engagement if
you are deliberate. Faster if you can shadow a live project.
