Excel mastery in finance is not about knowing every function, it is about
knowing the right handful deeply. Here are the seven that earn their keep in
almost every model I build.

## 1. XLOOKUP

The modern replacement for `VLOOKUP`. It looks left, returns arrays, and handles
"not found" gracefully.

```
=XLOOKUP(lookup_value, lookup_array, return_array, "Not found")
```

## 2. SUMIFS

Conditional totals are the backbone of management reporting. `SUMIFS` lets you
sum by account, cost centre, and period in one clean formula.

## 3. INDEX + MATCH

Still invaluable for two-way lookups and older workbooks where `XLOOKUP` is not
available.

## 4. IFERROR

Wrap anything fragile. A clean `IFERROR(..., 0)` keeps dashboards from breaking
on missing data.

## 5. TEXT

Format numbers and dates *inside* formulas, essential for building dynamic
report titles and labels.

## 6. EOMONTH

Period-end dates without manual edits. The unsung hero of any close calendar.

## 7. LET

Name intermediate calculations once and reuse them, faster, more readable
formulas that your future self will thank you for.

> Master these seven and you will out-build colleagues who "know" ten times as
> many functions but use none of them well.
