OTBI (Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence) is the reporting layer that
lets you build analyses directly on top of live Fusion data, no extract, no
warehouse, no waiting. For finance teams, it is the difference between *asking IT
for a report* and *answering your own question in five minutes*.

## The mental model

Everything in OTBI revolves around **subject areas**. A subject area is a
curated, business-friendly view of the data: facts (the numbers) and dimensions
(the ways you slice them).

> Pick the right subject area first. Ninety percent of OTBI frustration comes
> from starting in the wrong one.

## A simple first analysis

1. Open the **Reports and Analytics** work area.
2. Create a new analysis on the **General Ledger - Transactional Balances**
   subject area.
3. Drag in `Ledger`, `Accounting Period`, and `Amount`.
4. Add a filter for the current period and run it.

That is a working trial balance, built in minutes, refreshed live.

## Where people get stuck

- **Mixing grains.** Don't combine subject areas with incompatible levels of
  detail. If a number looks 10x too big, this is usually why.
- **Forgetting security.** OTBI respects data access sets. If a colleague sees
  different numbers, check their role assignments before debugging the report.

## Next steps

Once you are comfortable, graduate to **BI Publisher** for pixel-perfect,
printable output. OTBI for exploration, BIP for formal documents, that division
of labour will serve you well.
